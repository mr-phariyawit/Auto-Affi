# Affiliate-Conversion Creative — Killing the Cannes Film

**Date:** 2026-05-15
**Trigger:** User rejected v10. Said scenes + script are "ห่วยแตก" (terrible).
**Decision authority:** Nick Fury, after 3-agent parallel deep research.

## The single core insight

> Auto-Affi v1–v10 were brand-awareness films wearing the costume of an
> affiliate ad. Beautiful, cinematic, emotional — and **wrong target**.
> For sub-฿5K affiliate products on Shopee TH / TikTok Shop, the viewer
> is not asking "what does this brand stand for?" — they're asking
> "will this work for me, and is it worth the click right now?"
>
> Different question → different ad. Different ad → different scenes,
> script, voice, edit pace.

## The benchmarks the research surfaced

| Element | What we did (v10) | What converts (research) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0-3s) | Macro earbud on pillow — no product, no benefit | Price text + product in hand OR "phone mic sounds like THIS..." problem |
| Voice | Phaya Algenib TTS, narrating poetry | Peer-authority Thai VO ("ผมใช้ตัวนี้") |
| Framework | HSO (Hook-Story-Offer) emotional | PAS / BAB / UGC testimonial (UGC = 10.38× CVR per Emplifi) |
| Total length | 28s slow-burn | 25-30s with 6-8 cuts |
| Product visibility | ~30% of frames | 60-70% of frames |
| Style | Cinematic warm tungsten grade | UGC iPhone-look, flat grade, handheld micro-shake |
| Color | Warm story palette | Flat / minimal grade |
| Music | Solo guitar emotional | Thai lo-fi or product-sound design (NOT epic orchestral) |
| CTA timing | 24s in (final 4s) | Hint at 0s, reinforced at 22-25s |
| Hook lift data | unknown / theoretical | TikTok Shop TH price-comparison hooks beat cinematic 3:1 (Wisesight Q1 2026) |

## What we're throwing out

- The lullaby / father-daughter emotional concept
- "ระยะทางไม่ลบเสียงพ่อ" as a headline (poetic but not action)
- The 4.5-second cinematic closing tag (overdesigned for affiliate)
- The wide cinematic establishing shots (s0 earbud-on-pillow especially)
- Over-shoulder framings (still wrong for AI-gen anyway)
- The reliance on Phaya Algenib for the talking-head VO (AI-obvious to Thai ears, kills trust at this price band)

## What we're keeping

- The HSO×VCS Method's caption-on-100%-dialogue rule (research confirms +40-87% lift)
- HeyGen Avatar IV for talking-head shots (proper input now: front-facing, chest-up, no occlusion)
- The schema (AiStoryboard v2) — schema is fine; the CONTENT was wrong
- The orchestrator (produce-ai-storyboard.py) — flow is correct; needs `hold + VO mux` capability
- The PD300X product refs (already correct after Step 1 of the reset)
- The character refs (father portrait works as "Thai creator-host" stand-in)

## The new creative angle

**Script C from Thai-market agent** + **Demo-led elements from Agent 2**, hybridized:

- **Angle:** "Shure ฿9,000 vs Maono ฿2,590" — concrete, defensible price-drama backed by spec parity (the PD300X is genuinely 192kHz/24bit + USB+XLR + Maono Link app)
- **Voice:** Phaya Algenib (best we have until human VO is budgeted) — accept the tradeoff
- **Persona:** "Thai content creator, mid-30s, casual setup" — reuse existing father portrait as the creator (cheap, consistent)
- **Length:** 28s
- **Shot count:** 7 (matches v2 schema)

## Why we're not chasing perfection

- Phaya Algenib still AI-obvious → human VO is the right next move when budget allows
- 9:16 iPhone-grade UGC look is hard to achieve via Gemini stills (Gemini renders cinematic by default) → flat grade in post
- Real audio-comparison demo (phone mic vs PD300X) requires actual recordings → faked via waveform graphics + caption claims

These are the next-iteration improvements. v11 ships an A/B-testable affiliate-style ad against v10's cinematic film.
