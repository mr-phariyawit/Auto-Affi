# Benchmark Talent and Company Target Map

Date: 2026-06-04, Asia/Bangkok

Status: research synthesis for Auto-Affi's cinematic marketing + branded entertainment studio direction.

Purpose: a practical map of people and companies to study, approach, or benchmark while building a premium commercial film network. This is not a claim of current availability. It is a recruiting and learning radar.

Important currency note: Cannes Lions 2026 has not happened yet; the 2026 festival is scheduled for 2026-06-22 to 2026-06-26. Advertising-awards benchmarks should therefore use the completed 2025 cycle until 2026 winners are announced.

## Realism Labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Advisor | Plausible as a paid mentor, consultant, workshop guest, craft reviewer, or intro source. Usually easier through a company, rep, or former collaborator than cold direct outreach. |
| Project | Plausible for a specific commercial/branded film if the brief, budget, schedule, and producer are credible. |
| Benchmark | Study-only or long-term aspirational. Useful for taste calibration, pitch references, and internal standards, but not realistic near-term recruiting. |
| Gatekeeper | Company, rep, agency, or post house that can route access to multiple people. |

## Executive Read

Near-term recruiting should start with production companies, post houses, craft agencies, and Thailand/SEA service partners. The very top directors, DPs, editors, sound designers, VFX supervisors, composers, and creative directors are mostly benchmark targets until the company has brand clients, festival momentum, and repeatable production discipline.

The best first advisor profile is not "most famous person." It is a commercially experienced craft leader who can review treatments, bidding assumptions, casting approach, edit rhythm, sound plan, color pipeline, and post workflow before the company spends heavily.

Suggested near-term target mix:

- 2-3 Thailand/SEA production-service partners for practical project execution.
- 1 global commercial production-company producer as operating advisor.
- 1 editor or post supervisor as story/post advisor.
- 1 sound designer/mixer and 1 colorist as finish-quality advisors.
- 1 creative director with branded entertainment experience as pitch/taste advisor.
- A benchmark board of directors/DPs/editors/designers/composers whose work is studied, not recruited.

## Practical Recruiting Rule

For the first year, recruit advisors and collaborators around the bottlenecks that will actually break the work:

1. Producer who understands AICP-style bidding, agency/client politics, rights, and post.
2. Editor who can protect emotional clarity in 9:16 and 30s formats.
3. Sound designer/mixer who can make AI/generative source footage feel embodied and intentional.
4. Colorist/post supervisor who can create a repeatable finish pipeline.
5. Thai casting/production partner who can avoid generic performance and product-identity drift.

The star directors and Oscar-level craft names are invaluable as standards. The practical build path is through producers, reps, mid-tier craft artists, local production partners, and post houses.
