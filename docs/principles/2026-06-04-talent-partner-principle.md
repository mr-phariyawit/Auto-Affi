# Auto-Affi Talent and Partner Principle

Date: 2026-06-04

Purpose: turn Hollywood/company/talent research into an operating rule for building a cinematic commerce studio, not only an AI video shop.

## Core Rule

> Own the brief, strategy, taste, rights, approval, and learning loop. Partner for specialized craft until volume justifies hiring.

## Required Artifact

Every premium or repeated production workflow must include:

```text
talent_partner_plan.json
```

Affiliate MVP runs may keep this as a lightweight placeholder.

Required fields:

```text
run_id
profile
needed_roles
internal_owner
external_partner_candidates
why_partner_needed
rights_or_release_requirements
review_responsibilities
budget_band
outreach_status
notes
```

## Lean Core Roles

| Role | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Executive Producer / Founder | opportunity, budget, approvals, client/studio direction |
| Creative Strategy Lead | human truth, brand role, campaign architecture |
| AI Film Director | treatment, shot language, generation direction |
| Producer / Production Manager | schedule, run folders, providers, costs, vendors |
| Cinematography / Prompt Director | shot cards, lighting, keyframes, motion prompts |
| Post Supervisor | edit, sound, captions, color, cleanroom verification |
| Business Affairs / Compliance | claims, rights, disclosures, AI consent, publish blocks |
| Growth / Analytics Lead | subIds, dashboards, experiments, learning loop |

## Partner When

- the production needs live action, actors, complex locations, product table-top craft, VFX, color, sound, or legal/business affairs beyond internal capability;
- the project is for a client, premium brand, award/case film, or talent-facing campaign;
- production quality must be credited, insured, contracted, or released formally;
- an external craft partner reduces account, legal, or quality risk.

## Hard Gates

- Do not present AI generation as replacing craft ownership when a partner/talent contribution is material.
- Do not use external likeness, voice, work, reel, or reference beyond allowed scope.
- Do not approach talent/partners without a clear brief, rights scope, usage territory, and approval process.
- Do not frame Thailand as only cheap production; position it as premium Asian production capability.

## Review Rituals

| Ritual | Cadence | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt council | every generation | prevent weak/self-approved prompts |
| Dailies/review frames | every generation | catch product drift and craft issues |
| Postmortem | weekly or per run | turn failure/success into reusable playbooks |
| Partner review | monthly | identify which craft roles should be internalized |
