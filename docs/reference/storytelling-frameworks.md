## 3. How to pick (product / emotion → framework)

- **Tangible single pain point** (leaks, breaks, mess, wasted time) → **PAS** or **Problem-Solution Demo**. The proof shot IS the sell.
- **Need to overcome distrust / cheap-looking product** → **4 Ps** (proof beat) or **UGC** (peer authority). Best for unknown brands and sub-฿500.
- **Visible before/after** (skincare, cleaning, organizing) → **BAB** or **Transformation/Montage**.
- **Aspiration, not pain** (fashion, lifestyle, beauty glow-up) → **AIDA** or **Save-the-Cat**.
- **Novelty / "what is that?" gadget** → **Cold-Open/Trailer** for max scroll-stop.
- **Pure reach / launch, no depth** → **6-Second Ad**.
- **Spec-driven tech for informed buyers** → **FAB** (but expect low Shopee-feed fit).
- **Emotion ladder that works on TH feeds:** Curiosity → Frustration → Thrill/relief → Heart. Map it onto whatever spine you pick (this is exactly what the umbrella video does).
- **Avoid for 24s impulse:** Three-Act and SB7 — they need an attention budget a 24s muted autoplay short doesn't have.
