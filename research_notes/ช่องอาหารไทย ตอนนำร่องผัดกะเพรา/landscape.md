## 1. Who are the established English-language Thai cooking creators (platform, counts, format, sourcing depth, monetisation)?

### Takeaway
The English-language Thai-cooking slot is dominated by one specialist (Pailin's Kitchen, ~2.35M YouTube subs) plus generalist/adjacent giants (Mark Wiens ~12M, Andy Cooks ~6.4M, Marion's Kitchen ~2M) — all live-action, host-on-camera. Monetisation at the top is ads plus owned products (three Penguin Random House cookbooks for Pailin; a supermarket sauce/meal-kit company for Marion), not affiliate alone.

### Cited Findings
- **Pailin's Kitchen (formerly Hot Thai Kitchen), YouTube:** 2.35M subscribers, 264,912,743 total views, 832 videos, channel joined Sept 2009. `[FETCHED]` third-party tracker, data dated 2026-11-12 — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC27C_HWo-UmKkdWGsRJZ8EA)
- 832 videos over ~17 years ≈ 49 uploads/year ≈ about one a week (arithmetic on the vidIQ figures, not a stated schedule) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC27C_HWo-UmKkdWGsRJZ8EA)
- Most recent upload listed: a turkey video posted 2026-11-06 with 21.39K views after ~5 days — i.e. the channel now also covers non-Thai topics and a new long-form upload does roughly 1% of subscriber count in its first week. `[FETCHED]` — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC27C_HWo-UmKkdWGsRJZ8EA)

## 3. What do viewers of Thai cooking abroad repeatedly ask?

### Takeaway
The recurring, well-evidenced question is holy basil: what it is, how it differs from Thai basil, where to get it, and what to substitute — with authorities disagreeing among themselves (Italian basil vs Thai basil). Reddit and comment-section evidence could not be collected, so frequency is inferred from the number of recipe sites that pre-empt the question, not counted.

### Cited Findings
- Pailin (Saveur recipe) says Italian basil is a closer substitute for holy basil than Thai basil. `[SNIPPET]` — [Saveur](https://saveur.com/recipes/old-school-pad-gaprao-recipe)
- Pailin, 19 Jan 2024: "good old Italian basil will do the job also". `[FETCHED]` — [Hot Thai Kitchen Substack](https://hotthaikitchen.substack.com/p/the-last-pad-kra-pao-recipe-you-need)
- The Kitchn likewise says Italian basil is the better substitute over Thai basil. `[SNIPPET]` — [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/pad-krapow-recipe-23779855)
- Opposing view: Thai basil listed as "the most common swap", while saying substitution defeats the point of the dish. `[SNIPPET]` — [Ian Kewks](https://www.iankewks.com/pad-kra-pao-thai-holy-basil-stir-fry/)
- Rachel Cooks Thai: the two are not interchangeable but one can stand in for the other in a pinch; holy basil is hard to find; identify it by hairy stems and jagged leaf edges. `[SNIPPET]` — [Rachel Cooks Thai](https://rachelcooksthai.com/holy-basil/)
- Other suggested workarounds: Thai basil + mint, cinnamon basil; avoid dried basil. `[SNIPPET]`, blog-level — [Hip Hip Gourmet](https://hiphipgourmet.com/?p=36163); [Dorot Gardens](https://dorotgardens.com/substitute-for-thai-basil)
- "Grow your own" is repeated advice, including a forum thread titled "What's the best substitute for holy basil / gaprao in pad gaprao?". `[SNIPPET]` (thread returned 403 on fetch) — [ASEAN NOW](https://aseannow.com/topic/1240384-whats-the-best-substitute-for-holy-basil-gaprao-in-pad-gaprao/); [Shers He Grows](https://shershegrows.com/pad-kra-pao-thai-basil-stir-fry/)
- Naming/romanisation confusion is itself a FAQ: Pailin uses "pad kra pao" in titles and "pad gaprao" in speech and maintains a spelling FAQ. `[FETCHED]` — [Hot Thai Kitchen Substack](https://hotthaikitchen.substack.com/p/the-last-pad-kra-pao-recipe-you-need)
- Retailer/explainer pages exist purely to disambiguate holy vs Thai vs sweet basil. `[SNIPPET]` — [Oriental Mart UK](https://www.orientalmart.co.uk/blog/holy-basil-vs-thai-basil); [SAYS](https://says.com/my/lifestyle/eats/did-you-know-sweet-basil-is-not-thai-basil-and-holy-basil-is-a-whole-other-thing)
- Allergy substitution appears in comments: a reader asks for a fish-sauce substitute safe for shrimp allergy (one comment, Jan 2024). `[FETCHED]` — [Hot Thai Kitchen Substack](https://hotthaikitchen.substack.com/p/the-last-pad-kra-pao-recipe-you-need)

### Inferences
- Even the experts disagree on the holy-basil substitute; a channel that shows the varieties visually (leaf shape, stem hair, colour) and ranks substitutes with reasons addresses a real, unresolved confusion. Illustration suits botanical identification.
- Romanisation variance (kra pao / krapow / gaprao / kaprao) is a search and comprehension problem — relevant to bilingual titling.

### Gaps
- **Reddit (r/ThaiFood, r/Cooking, r/AskCulinary): no evidence collected** — the domain is blocked to the tools used. Needs a manual read.
- YouTube comment sections: not accessible; no comment-frequency evidence.
- Fish-sauce brand questions (Megachef, Squid, Tiparos etc.): no source found in this pass; not evidenced.
- Pailin's own holy-basil and FAQ pages: HTTP 402, unread.

## 4. Platform economics and policy, 2026

### Takeaway
YouTube is the only platform with verified, officially documented eligibility for this format: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours/12 mo or 10M Shorts views/90 days today, doubling to 8,000 hours / 20M Shorts views for new applicants from 1 Feb 2027. AI-assisted non-photorealistic animation needs no realism disclosure and is monetisable if it shows original insight rather than templated mass production; TikTok Creator Rewards eligibility for Thailand could not be confirmed from any official page.

### Cited Findings
**YouTube Partner Program**
- Current: 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 12 months, OR 1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days; Shorts-feed watch time does not count toward the 4,000 hours; meeting thresholds does not guarantee acceptance (policy review). `[OFFICIAL]`, fetched 2026-11-13 — [YouTube Help: YPP overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)
- The same page announces YPP updates starting 1 February 2027, with updated terms to accept in Studio by 31 January 2027. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)
- Reported detail of the Feb 2027 change (new applicants only; existing partners grandfathered): 8,000 watch hours/365 days OR 20M Shorts views/90 days; and all partners need 10M Shorts views/90 days to earn Shorts ad revenue. Fan-funding tier unchanged: 500 subs, 3 uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 watch hours/yr or 3M Shorts views/90 days. `[FETCHED]`, trade press dated 2026-08-10, not the official page — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/)

**YouTube "inauthentic content" (15 July 2025)**
- The policy formerly called "repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content" on 15 July 2025, clarified as covering "repetitive or mass-produced" content, which YouTube says was always ineligible. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Not monetisable (examples): near-identical videos with minimal variation; image slideshows, templated storylines or scrolling text with little narrative/educational value; AI-generated content from generic templates without the creator's original insight. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Allowed (examples): same intro/outro with differing main content; a series where each video has a distinct focus; using AI to visualise a character/narrative you invented, edit scripts, or generate a background visual, provided the result reflects your creative vision. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Channels using AI personas presenting as human experts on health/legal/financial/political topics cannot monetise. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- The policy page does not mention animation, synthetic voice or text-to-speech by name. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Reported: policy is assessed at channel level, not per video. `[SNIPPET]`, vendor blog — [subsub.io](https://www.subsub.io/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2025)

**YouTube altered/synthetic disclosure**
- Disclosure is required for realistic altered/synthetic content (real person saying/doing what they didn't, altered real events/places, realistic scenes that didn't happen, AI music as main focus). `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube Help: Disclosing altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- Not required for clearly unrealistic content (example given: AI-generated element in a fully animated video), nor for AI used for scripts, outlines, thumbnails, titles, infographics, captions, upscaling, or cloning your own voice for voiceover/dubs. An AI label may still appear in the expanded description for animated content. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- Consistent non-disclosure can lead to YouTube applying labels, content removal or YPP suspension; disclosure by itself does not limit audience or monetisation. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- The page does not address a synthetic voice that is not your own clone. `[OFFICIAL]` — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

**TikTok Creator Rewards Program** (all third-party; official page unreadable)
- Commonly reported requirements: age 18+, 10,000 followers, videos longer than one minute, original content; one guide adds 100,000 views in the last 30 days. `[SNIPPET]` — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-creativity-program/); [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/)
- Commonly reported regions: US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Brazil (one guide adds Mexico). Thailand is not on these lists. `[SNIPPET]`, unofficial; one source notes TikTok publishes no definitive list — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/); [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- Thailand WAS added (Mar 2024) to the separate *Effect Creator Rewards* program for Effect House effects — not the video rewards program. `[SNIPPET]` — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/03/13/tiktok-effect-creator-rewards-monetization-program-requirements/)

**Instagram**
- Reported tools (Oct 2026): Subscriptions (10,000 followers), Gifts (500 followers), Badges, Partnership Ads, Creator Marketplace, Bonuses (invite-only, no application path). `[SNIPPET]`, third-party — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-monetization-requirements/)
- An older Meta Creators page lists Thailand among countries where Gifts are available, citing a 5,000-follower minimum (conflicts with the 500 figure). `[SNIPPET]`, undated — [Meta for Creators](https://creators.facebook.com/giving-more-creators-around-the-world-ways-to-earn-money)

### Inferences
- A channel started now and not accepted into YPP before 1 Feb 2027 faces the doubled bar (8,000 h / 20M Shorts views). That is ~11 weeks from the research date — realistically the new bar applies.
- After Feb 2027 a Shorts-only strategy earns Shorts ad revenue only above 10M views/90 days; long-form watch hours become the more attainable path for an instructional channel.
- For this format on YouTube: illustrated, clearly non-photorealistic animation → no realism disclosure required; monetisation risk is the "templated/mass-produced" test, so each episode needs distinct research, narration and insight. Detailed sourcing content is the defence.
- If TikTok Creator Rewards is unavailable to Thailand-based accounts (unconfirmed), TikTok/Instagram function as reach + affiliate/shop channels, not ad-revenue channels.

### Gaps
- Official TikTok Creator Rewards eligibility (countries, one-minute rule wording): official pages returned empty; nothing official cited. Operator should check Creator Tools in-app.
- YouTube's official blog post with the Feb 2027 numbers was not opened; numbers rest on Tubefilter.
- YouTube's treatment of TTS / third-party synthetic voice under monetisation and disclosure: not stated on the official pages read.
- Instagram official eligibility by country for Thailand in 2026: no current official page retrieved.
- TikTok and Meta AI-content labelling rules: not researched in this pass.
- RPM/earnings figures: none collected (no proven source).

## 5. Realistic production methods for detailed illustrated animation by one person, 2026

### Takeaway
I found documentation of the tools' limits and prices but no first-hand solo-creator workflow write-up for illustrated recipe animation. What the evidence supports: image-to-video models are usable for ambient cooking motion but hands remain the known failure point, so the lower-risk design is illustrated stills with layered/parallax motion and hands kept out of AI-generated shots.

### Cited Findings
- Precedent, traditional: recipe illustrations animated in Photoshop to reveal steps (illustrator portfolio). — [Jennifer Hines](https://jenniferhines.myportfolio.com/animated-recipe-illustration-series)
- Precedent, 3D: ChouChou — Blender for characters/animation/render, After Effects for compositing, made by a professional feature-animation director. `[FETCHED]` — [BlenderNation](https://www.blendernation.com/2021/08/25/watch-stylish-cooking-videos-made-by-minions-director-using-blender/)
- Hands and faces are described as the failures viewers notice fastest in AI video, attributed to training-data gaps. `[SNIPPET]`, vendor blog — [Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-video-hands-faces)
- Suggested mitigations: use slow motion, compose shots to avoid hands, or composite replacements. `[SNIPPET]` — [Dan Taylor-Watt, AI video supertest](https://dantaylorwatt.substack.com/p/ai-video-supertest-e11); [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/how-to-fix-warped-hands-and-faces-in-ai-generated-video)
- Kling's own cooking-video page positions its output as the cooking process rather than a finished-plate hero shot. `[SNIPPET]`, vendor — [Kling](https://kling.ai/topics/ai-cooking-video)
- Model comparisons disagree (Veo 3.1 overall winner in one test; Kling better in another; "best parallax: Kling 2.5" in a roundup). `[SNIPPET]` — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/323056/best-short-form-ai-video-generator-kling-google-veo); [Scenario: choosing a video model 2026](https://help.scenario.com/articles/6618023406-how-to-choose-the-right-video-model-2026-edition)
- Published prices (third-party compilations of vendor rates; they conflict): Runway Gen-4 Turbo 5 credits/s and Gen-4.5 12 credits/s at $0.01/credit (≈$0.60 per 5 s on Gen-4.5); Veo 3.1 Lite $0.03/s at 720p, Veo 3.1 Standard $0.40/s; Kling 3.0 720p cited at both $0.084/s and $0.13/s. `[SNIPPET]` — [invideo pricing roundup, Aug 2026](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-video-model-pricing); [Developers Digest, Oct 2026](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/image-video-generation-api-pricing-2026); [Metronome: Runway API](https://metronome.com/pricing-index/runway-api)
- Midjourney video: paid plans from $10/month; V1 video reported at ~8x the compute of an image, roughly one image-equivalent per second. `[SNIPPET]` — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/de/news/midjourney-v1-ai-video-model/)

### Inferences
- At the cited rates, 60 s of generated motion costs roughly $1.80 (Veo 3.1 Lite) to $24 (Veo 3.1 Standard) *per accepted take*; rejected takes multiply this. No source gave an acceptance rate for food shots, so real cost per finished minute is unknown.
- A hybrid is the defensible plan: (a) illustration consistency from style reference + ingredient/object sheets; (b) parallax/cut-out motion for most shots; (c) image-to-video only for hand-free ambient shots (steam, sizzle, pour). This is my synthesis, not a documented creator workflow.
- Frame-by-frame at "detailed illustration" quality is not supported by any solo precedent found; the one high-craft example (ChouChou) is a professional director.

### Gaps
- No creator write-up found for a solo illustrated-recipe animation pipeline (time per episode, cost per episode).
- No documentation retrieved for style-reference / character-and-object-sheet consistency workflows (search returned only Midjourney video pricing).
- No evidence specific to AI failure modes on *food* (liquids, steam, chopped herbs, text on packaging) — only general hands/faces claims from vendor blogs.
- No sourced cost or time for cut-out rigging / After Effects parallax.
- All prices are third-party compilations; vendor pricing pages were not opened.

## 6. Bilingual presentation conventions and on-screen text legibility

### Takeaway
No official platform guidance on bilingual captions exists in what I found; the best available convention is stacked two-line captions, one language per line, each following its own line-length rule and distinguished by colour. Vertical safe-zone figures are vendor-derived and conflict, so they must be checked in each app's preview.

### Cited Findings
- Bilingual stacked cues: each language on its own line following its own length rule; differentiate by colour (e.g. white primary, pale yellow secondary). `[SNIPPET]`, subtitle-tool vendor citing Hong Kong practice — [Subanana](https://subanana.com/en-HK/blog/four-points-to-note-when-creating-video-subtitle)
- Meta/Google/TikTok publish safe-zone resources mainly for ads; they should not be treated as fixed pixel rules for organic posts. `[SNIPPET]` — [Reap](https://reap.video/blog/short-form-video-safe-zones)
- Conflicting safe-zone figures on 1080×1920: caption band ~55% to 75–80% from top; platform UI covers the bottom 25–30%; a "universal" 900×1400 px zone; Reels 1010×1280 px zone set 220 px from top and 420 px from bottom. `[SNIPPET]`, vendor blogs — [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/caption-placement-guide.md); [Ignite Social Media](https://www.ignitesocialmedia.com/content-creation/what-are-the-safe-zones-for-tiktoks-and-instagram-reels/); [Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/vertical-video-safe-zone/)
- UI overlays are not in the exported file, so preview after upload; keep an editable SRT for translation/accessibility. `[SNIPPET]` — [Subclip](https://subclip.app/blogs/animated-captions-2026-best-practices-reels-shorts-x-exporting-srt)
- Broadcast norms often cited: max two lines; Netflix English 42 characters/line; teletext-derived 37 characters; reading speed 160–180 wpm. Sources disagree on whether the BBC sets a character cap (one says 68% of 16:9 width, another says no cap). `[SNIPPET]`, secondary — [Clevercast on BBC guidelines](https://clevercast.com/bbc-subtitling-guidelines); [Subtitle reading speed guide](https://subtitle-toolset.pages.dev/guides/subtitle-reading-speed/)
- Romanisation is inconsistent even within one leading creator's output (kra pao vs gaprao), handled via an FAQ. `[FETCHED]` — [Hot Thai Kitchen Substack](https://hotthaikitchen.substack.com/p/the-last-pad-kra-pao-recipe-you-need)

### Inferences
- A bilingual stack needs two lines minimum, so with a two-line maximum each language gets one short line — narration must be written short, or one language carried by voice and the other by text.
- For ingredient names a three-part label (Thai script / romanisation / English) is a design opportunity for an illustrated format, since labels can be part of the artwork rather than captions. Pick one romanisation and list variants once.

### Gaps
- No examples of successful bilingual channels analysed (none retrieved); how they split voice vs text is unevidenced.
- No Thai-script-specific legibility guidance (tone marks/vowel stacking need extra line height) found from a citable source.
- No WCAG or primary BBC/Netflix documents opened; contrast and minimum-size figures not sourced.
- No official organic safe-zone spec from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube retrieved.
