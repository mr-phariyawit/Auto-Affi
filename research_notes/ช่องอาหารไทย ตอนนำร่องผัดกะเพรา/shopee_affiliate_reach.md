# Shopee Thailand affiliate links for an audience outside Thailand: who can buy, where it ships, what it pays, how to present it

Research date: 2026-11-13. Tags: **[P]** = official page opened and read by me (logged out); **[S]** = secondary source; **[U]** = search snippet only, page not read / unverified.
Thai Buddhist-era dates on Shopee pages are converted (2568 = 2025, 2569 = 2026).
Method note: Shopee help-centre pages are JavaScript apps; they were read in a logged-out browser tab. No login, sign-up, form submission or purchase flow was used.

---

## 1. Shopee markets in 2026, and whether a buyer in the US/UK/EU/Australia/Canada/Japan can buy from a Thai seller

### Takeaway
Shopee Thailand's own pages describe a country-locked service: the Thai app is for use in Thailand, foreign phone numbers are accepted only from 10 Southeast Asian countries, and there is no US/UK/EU/AU/CA/JP Shopee site listed. I found no Shopee Thailand page offering delivery to an address outside Thailand; the only official cross-border route out of Thailand is the seller-side SIP programme (section 2), which reaches Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines and Laos.

### Cited Findings
- [P] The footer of shopee.co.th (read 2026-11-13, logged out) lists "Country & Region": Argentina, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, Philippines, Brazil, Mexico, Taiwan. No US, UK, EU, Australia, Canada or Japan site is listed. — [shopee.co.th](https://shopee.co.th/)
- [P] Shopee Thailand's help article "Which countries does Shopee have?" says Shopee operates in Southeast Asia, Taiwan, Europe and Latin America and refers readers to the site footer for the current list. (The "Europe" wording conflicts with the footer list above, which has no European site; the article looks older than the footer.) — [help.shopee.co.th article 80892](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/80892)
- [P] Same article, note: "You can use the Shopee app only within the country where you activated it. If you want to order from another country's Shopee and that shop has no international shipping service, you must register the Shopee app with a phone number of that country in order to place the order." — [help.shopee.co.th article 80892](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/80892)
- [P] "You cannot change the country used in the Shopee app. To view or buy products from another country you must use the Shopee app specific to that country (e.g. Shopee TH App for Thailand) … sign up for a new account using a phone number belonging to that country." — [help.shopee.co.th article 80455](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/80455)
- [P] Since 6 March 2025, foreign users can register a Shopee Thailand account with foreign phone numbers from exactly 10 countries: Cambodia, Laos, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Brunei, East Timor, Vietnam. No US, UK, EU, Australian, Canadian or Japanese numbers are listed. — [help.shopee.co.th article 176286](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/176286)
- [P] Same article: refunds for cancelled/returned orders, unless the original payment was by credit card, can only be made to a Thai bank account. — [help.shopee.co.th article 176286](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/176286)
- [P] Searching the Shopee Thailand buyer help centre for "ส่งไปต่างประเทศ" (ship abroad), "ที่อยู่จัดส่งนอกประเทศไทย" (delivery address outside Thailand) and "international shipping overseas" returned only articles about orders shipped *into* Thailand from overseas sellers, the foreign-phone article, the SIP terms and the country articles above. No article about delivering a Thai order to an overseas address appeared in the first page of results for any of the three queries. — [help.shopee.co.th search](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/search?keyword=international%20shipping%20overseas)

### Inferences
- A viewer in the US, UK, EU, Australia, Canada or Japan cannot realistically complete a purchase from a Shopee Thailand link: they cannot register with their own phone number (not among the 10 accepted countries), there is no Shopee site for their country, and no outbound-delivery option is documented. This is "not offered on any page I could find", not a quoted prohibition.
- A foreigner physically in Thailand (tourist, expat) or a person with a Thai/ASEAN phone number and a Thai delivery address can buy; that is the realistic non-Thai buyer for a Shopee TH link.
- A viewer in Singapore, Malaysia or the Philippines would buy on their own country's Shopee, not on shopee.co.th (see section 2).

### Gaps
- Whether the Shopee TH checkout address form technically accepts a non-Thai address could not be checked: it is behind login, which was out of scope.
- No Shopee Thailand page states in so many words "we do not deliver outside Thailand"; the conclusion rests on the country-lock statements and the absence of any outbound option.
- The phrase "and that shop has no international shipping service" in article 80892 implies some shops may offer one; I found no page describing a buyer-facing international shipping option on Shopee Thailand other than SIP.
- Shopee market list was confirmed only from the Shopee Thailand footer. I did not open each country site to confirm it is trading in November 2026.

## 2. SIP: where a Thai seller's listing is syndicated, and whether a Thai affiliate link earns on purchases made in another country's Shopee

### Takeaway
Shopee Thailand's Seller Education Hub (page dated 16 Oct 2026) says SIP orders come from customers in Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines and Laos, with no plan to expand and Vietnam suspended from 23 Oct 2026; joining is by invitation only. The listing appears as a separate Shopee-managed shop on the foreign site at a Shopee-set local-currency price, so a shopee.co.th affiliate link does not point at it; I found no statement that a Thai affiliate link earns commission on a foreign-site SIP order.

### Cited Findings
- [P] "Orders will come from customers in Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines and Laos. At present the SIP programme has no plan to expand to other countries. (From 23 October 2026 the programme temporarily suspends sales to Vietnam…)" Page date shown: 16-10-2026. — [Shopee TH Seller Education Hub, article 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] How it works: the Thai seller ships domestically to a Shopee-designated warehouse in Thailand under the buyer name "thsip.buyer"; Shopee then ships abroad. Shop creation abroad, product/stock management, pricing, buyer chat and international shipping are done by Shopee. The overseas buyer pays the shipping. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] Joining is by invitation only (update dated 25 Dec 2025): eligible shops get a pop-up in Seller Centre; there is no application form. Most shops were invited from 2022. Criteria include: shop located in Thailand, normal status, at least 2 completed orders in 30 days, cancellation rate at most 3% over 90 days, open at least 30 days, shop rating at least 4, not a shop selling tickets/vouchers or plants. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] Pricing: Shopee sets the price shown in the overseas shop taking account of local currency, shipping and other costs; the seller normally receives the same as for a domestic sale. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] SIP Terms of Service: by joining, the seller authorises Shopee to display the shop and products on "overseas sites" on the seller's behalf; all products in the Thai shop are shown abroad automatically unless (i) restricted by Shopee, (ii) restricted by Thai law or the law of the overseas site's country, or (iii) the seller opts a product out. Price abroad may be adjusted by Shopee for logistics cost, import duty and customs tax, exchange rate, and service fees, and is shown in local currency. The terms do not name the destination countries. — [help.shopee.co.th article 137206](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/137206)
- [P] A listing may fail to appear abroad if it is a SIP-prohibited product or does not meet destination-country rules, is in a banned or no-sales category, has been listed abroad for more than 90 days with no sales, has Thai stock below 2, or has a rating below 3.5. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] Internal inconsistency on the same page: the intro names four countries (Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Laos), the shipping step also mentions Vietnam, the prohibited-items note says "all 3 destination countries", and the agent chat account names cover my/sg/ph plus a Taiwan account (sip_official_tw_cs_3.th). — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] Thai affiliate T&C (revised 20 Sep 2026, effective 1 Oct 2026) define a commissionable "Completed Purchase" as a transaction completed and confirmed through "the Platform" as a direct result of the buyer reaching the Platform through the affiliate link; "Platform" is defined as any platform provided by Shopee including its apps and websites; "Territory" is the country where the Shopee entity engaging the affiliate is located. The text does not address purchases made on another country's Shopee site. — [help.shopee.co.th article 77257](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77257)
- [S] Sea Ltd press release (9 Aug 2023): SIP with Thailand's DITP let participating entrepreneurs export to Singapore, building on a similar programme for Malaysia; Shopee handles shop set-up, product and inventory management, buyer communication and shipping at no additional fee. — [Sea news 301](https://www.sea.com/news/301)

### Inferences
- A link generated in the Shopee Thailand affiliate programme opens a shopee.co.th product page. A SIP sale happens on a different site (for example shopee.sg), in a separate Shopee-run shop, at a different price, and reaches the Thai seller as an order from "thsip.buyer". Nothing I read connects that foreign-site order to a Thai affiliate click, so the working assumption should be: no commission.
- The creator cannot tell from the Thai listing whether a given product is actually live on the Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines site; it depends on the seller's SIP status and the filters above.
- For viewers in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines the same Thai product may be purchasable locally, but the creator would need that country's own product URL (and that country's affiliate programme, section 3) to earn from it.

### Gaps
- No official statement was found either way on whether a Shopee TH affiliate link earns on a purchase made on another country's Shopee. Treat as unconfirmed; the answer above is inference.
- I did not open any SIP listing on shopee.sg / shopee.com.my / shopee.ph to see how a Thai seasoning listing actually appears, what it costs, or how long delivery takes.
- Whether Taiwan is currently a SIP destination for Thai sellers is unclear (account name suggests yes, country list says no).

## 3. Shopee Affiliate Program Thailand: eligibility, commission, attribution, payout, posting and disclosure rules; programmes in other Shopee markets

### Takeaway
For an individual affiliate, Shopee Thailand pays 2.00% on direct orders and 0.60% on indirect orders in Food & Beverages (same as most categories), capped at 225 baht per order, on a 7-day last-click basis, paid out twice a week once 100 baht is accrued and KYC is valid; payment requires a bank account located in Thailand. Shopee affiliate programmes also exist in Singapore and Malaysia (their affiliate help centres were opened); Shopee Singapore steers applicants whose audience is not mainly in Singapore to their own market's team.

### Cited Findings
**Commission**
- [P] Rate table for individual affiliates, social-media channel, existing customers, effective 20 April 2026: Food & Beverages 2.00% direct / 0.60% indirect; the same 2.00% / 0.60% applies to nearly all listed categories; Utilities, Donation, Local Service and Investment pay 0%. Footnote: maximum 225 baht per order. New-customer rates are on a separate linked page (not read). — [help.shopee.co.th affiliate article 123898](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/123898)
- [P] Direct order = the linked product, or another product from the same shop; pays Shopee commission plus seller commission where the seller has switched on "Extra Comm". Indirect order = a product from a different shop; pays 30% of the direct rate, counted for at most one indirect order within 7 days of the click. — [affiliate article 164346](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/164346)
- [P] The commission base is the "Net Completed Purchase Value": the completed purchase total minus discounts, shipping fees, voucher fees and rebates such as Shopee Coins. — [Affiliate T&C, article 77257](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77257)
- [P] The help article "How commission is calculated" currently contains only "TBC...". — [affiliate article 183361](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/183361)

**Attribution**
- [P] Last-click attribution; the order must occur within 7 days of the click. Priority: last click on the promoted product, then last click on a product from the same shop. — [affiliate article 164346](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/164346)

**Payout**
- [P] Individuals not VAT-registered: paid twice a week (new bills only). Companies and VAT-registered individuals: twice a month. Conditions: payment and tax information (KYC) verified as "Valid", and accrued commission of at least 100 baht. Bank processing may add 1 to 2 days. — [affiliate article 142437](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/142437)
- [P] T&C: participation "is limited to those who can receive payment through a bank account located in Thailand, whether the participant resides in Thailand or abroad"; an affiliate who cannot open a Thai bank account or lacks valid Thai tax documents is not eligible to join or be paid. — [Affiliate T&C, article 77257](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77257)

**Joining**
- [P] Apply on the web (shopee.co.th/m/affiliate-influencer) or in the Shopee app; connect at least one social channel (TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube or Facebook), give phone and email, confirm by email OTP; confirmation email in 1 to 2 working days. No follower minimum is stated on this page. — [affiliate article 123889](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/123889)

**Where links may be posted; prohibited content and products**
- [P] Affiliate links for products, shops and campaigns "can be posted on every social media platform" of the affiliate; examples shown are Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and X. — [affiliate article 141915](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/141915)
- [P] T&C prohibited acts include using advertising email to promote Shopee without Shopee's prior written consent, automated query tools, and fraudulent search-engine marketing. Prohibited content includes content unrelated to the product advertised by the affiliate link (spamming) and IP-infringing content. — [Affiliate T&C, article 77257](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77257)
- [P] Products that earn no commission if promoted: certain jewellery (investment precious metals, rings, earrings, bracelets, anklets, charms and pendants), weapons, cigarettes/tobacco, live animals and carcasses, services (Utilities, Donation, Local Service, Investment), and all milk powder. Food seasonings are not on the list. — [affiliate article 132895](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/132895)
- [P] The affiliate help centre has a policy section on prohibited content (gambling, spam, sexual content, drugs/weapons/tobacco, clickbait, politics/religion/race, sensitive content, copied content) and a guideline on using the Shopee and Shopee Affiliate logos. I read the titles only, not each article. — [Shopee Affiliate Help Center TH](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10)

**Disclosure**
- [P] In the T&C passages I searched (keyword "เปิดเผย" = disclose), the disclosure language concerns Shopee disclosing the affiliate's data to sellers, not a duty on the affiliate to label posts as affiliate content. I did not read all ~71,000 characters of the T&C. — [Affiliate T&C, article 77257](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77257)

**Platform-integrated variants offered by Shopee Thailand**
- [P] YouTube Shopping Affiliate (Shopee TH): tag Shopee products in videos, Shorts and live streams; requirements listed by Shopee are YouTube Partner Program membership, more than 10,000 subscribers, not a music or made-for-kids channel, no Community Guidelines violations; up to 30 products per video. — [affiliate article 164376](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/164376)
- [P] Instagram Affiliate Partnership (Shopee TH): tag Shopee products in feed posts and Reels; requires age 18+, a professional Instagram account, at least 1,000 followers, compliance with Instagram monetisation policies, and registration in the Shopee Affiliate Program. — [affiliate article 203759](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/203759)

**Other Shopee markets**
- [P] Shopee Singapore has its own Affiliate Help Center and programme. Its FAQ answers "Can I apply for the SG Shopee Affiliate Program if I'm not based in Singapore/currently residing overseas?" with: "Our team handles SG affiliates and if your readers are not mainly from SG, we would recommend you reach out to the respective market Shopee Affiliates Team instead." — [help.shopee.sg affiliate article 126383](https://help.shopee.sg/portal/10/article/126383)
- [P] Shopee Malaysia has its own Affiliate Help Center (categories: Affiliate Account & Earnings, Affiliate Features & Programmes, Content Playbook & Guidelines, Help & Policies). I opened the portal and a search-results page only; no eligibility article was read. — [help.shopee.com.my affiliate portal](https://help.shopee.com.my/portal/10)
- [U] A search snippet attributes to Shopee Malaysia's help centre a 7-day last-click window and a base commission cap of RM5 per completed order. The article itself would not load for me (redirected to the portal home). — [help.shopee.com.my article 140905](https://help.shopee.com.my/portal/10/article/140905)
- [U] Involve Asia's directory lists a "Shopee TH – CPS" offer with a 7-day cookie; the page content did not load, so its rates are unverified. — [Involve Asia directory](https://app.involve.asia/directory/shopee-thailand-cps-affiliate-program)

### Inferences
- Worked example at the published rate: a 100-baht bottle of fish sauce bought through a direct link yields about 2 baht before any seller Extra Comm, and less after vouchers and coins are deducted from the base. The 225-baht cap is irrelevant at seasoning prices.
- A Thailand resident with a Thai bank account fits the Thai programme. Joining Singapore's or Malaysia's programme would, by analogy with the Thai T&C, probably hinge on being payable in that country; that is an assumption, not something I read for those markets.
- The Singapore FAQ answer is a redirect, not a refusal, and it turns on where the audience is. A Thai creator whose viewers are mostly in Singapore is not obviously excluded, but the page does not confirm acceptance.

### Gaps
- Bank/tax/residency requirements for the Singapore, Malaysia and Philippines affiliate programmes were not read. Whether a Thailand resident can be paid by them is unconfirmed.
- Whether affiliate programmes exist on Shopee Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, Taiwan, Brazil, Mexico and Argentina was not checked on their own pages.
- New-customer commission rates, Extra Comm rates for specific seasoning brands, and the service-fee article (177422) were not read.
- No Shopee Thailand page was found that requires or forbids an "affiliate link" disclosure in posts; Thai consumer-protection or advertising law on this was out of scope and not researched.

## 4. Platform rules for external shopping links: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram

### Takeaway
TikTok's own support page requires the commercial content disclosure setting whenever a post promotes a third-party brand or product in exchange for payment or any other incentive; YouTube's policy explicitly says affiliate content does not violate its terms but excessive affiliate posting can be treated as spam. I could not read official pages for TikTok's link-in-bio threshold or Instagram's rules, so those remain secondary or open.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok**
- [P] Promotional content covers "promoting a third-party brand or its products or services in exchange for payment or any other incentive." "When posting content that promotes a brand, product, or service on TikTok, you must turn on the content disclosure setting." Without proper disclosure "we may remove or restrict your posts." — [TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088170904085)
- [P] Promoting your own brand is labelled "Promotional content"; branded content for another business is labelled "Paid partnership". The label cannot be changed after publishing. Turning the setting on "won't affect the distribution of your post". — [TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088170904085)
- [S] Third-party guides agree that a personal account needs about 1,000 followers before the clickable website field appears in the bio, and that business accounts have a separate route; they disagree on whether registration proof is needed and say it varies by region. A URL typed into bio text without the field is plain text, not clickable. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-to-put-a-link-in-tiktok-bio/); [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-to-add-link-to-tiktok-bio)

**YouTube**
- [P] External links policy: "Affiliate content doesn't violate YouTube's Terms of Use." However, "Excessively posting affiliate content in dedicated accounts may violate our policies around spam." The policy also notes "Certain links may not be clickable." Links to sites selling regulated goods are not allowed. — [YouTube Help 9054257](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9054257?hl=en)
- [P] Paid promotion: creators with paid product placements, sponsorships or endorsements must tick the paid promotion box in video details, which adds a disclosure label; creators remain responsible for complying with applicable law (the page points to the FTC and the UK ASA). The page does not mention affiliate links specifically. — [YouTube Help 154235](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en)
- [P] YouTube Shopping affiliate programme is available to eligible creators based in: Argentina, Brazil, Germany, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, Philippines, Poland, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, United Kingdom, United States and Vietnam. Requirements: in the YouTube Partner Program, meets the YPP subscriber threshold, not a music or made-for-kids channel. The page does not list retailers per country or say what viewers in other countries see. — [YouTube Help 13376398](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en)
- [P] Shopee Thailand's own description of the same programme states a higher bar: more than 10,000 subscribers. (Conflict with the YouTube page, which says only "meets the subscriber threshold for YPP".) — [Shopee affiliate article 164376](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/164376)

**Instagram**
- [P] Shopee Thailand states that its Instagram Affiliate Partnership needs a professional account, age 18+, at least 1,000 followers and compliance with Instagram's monetisation rules. This is Shopee's description, not Meta's page. — [Shopee affiliate article 203759](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/203759)

### Inferences
- An affiliate commission is an "incentive", so on TikTok a video that points viewers to a Shopee affiliate link falls within the wording of the disclosure requirement; choose the third-party ("Branded content") option rather than "Your brand".
- YouTube description links are the lowest-friction placement: no follower threshold was found in the policy, and affiliate links are expressly tolerated in moderation.
- Product tagging through YouTube Shopping or Instagram Affiliate Partnership would surface Shopee Thailand products to a largely non-Thai audience who cannot buy them; these integrations do not solve the delivery problem.

### Gaps
- TikTok's official link-in-bio eligibility page was not found; the 1,000-follower figure is secondary only.
- TikTok's official position on external marketplace links versus TikTok Shop (for example, whether links to competing marketplaces are restricted in bio or captions, in Thailand or elsewhere) was not found. Not confirmed either way.
- Instagram/Meta's own pages on link placement and on labelling affiliate content were not read (the help page I reached was an index of partnership-ads articles only).
- Which YouTube links are clickable for a new or small channel (description, Shorts, comments) was not read from the official article that the policy page points to.
- Whether viewers outside Thailand see Shopee TH product tags in YouTube Shopping or Instagram is not stated on the pages read.

## 5. Product-side constraints: are sauces and dried spices allowed in Shopee's cross-border programme?

### Takeaway
Shopee Thailand's SIP page bans fresh food, frozen food, alcoholic drinks, "plants and related products", and anything needing an overseas sales licence; it does not name shelf-stable sauces or dried spices as banned, but adds that every other category is assessed by Shopee per destination country and that breakables are "not recommended". So bottled sauces and dried spices are not excluded on paper, yet nothing confirms they are actually carried.

### Cited Findings
- [P] SIP-prohibited products "such as": anything not sellable on Shopee Thailand; bulky goods and large furniture; plants and related products; living things; frozen food; fresh food; alcoholic beverages; second-hand cosmetics; medicine; contact lenses; pesticides and chemicals; flammable chemicals and materials; adult products; weapons; cigarettes and e-cigarettes; power banks and battery goods; goods needing a licence to sell abroad; credit/debit cards; tickets, vouchers and services; money or equivalents such as gold and diamonds; counterfeit goods; cannabis; pets. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] "The categories above cannot be sold in all 3 destination countries. Other categories will be considered by Shopee for each destination country as to their suitability." — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] For supplements, cosmetics or other goods needing a licence at home and/or abroad, the seller need not register for extra licences; products Shopee has screened out as not listable abroad simply are not uploaded to the destination. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] Packing guidance: "Shipping fragile items is not recommended"; weight and size limits apply per destination country (the limits table was an image and was not read). — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)
- [P] Shops selling plants are excluded from SIP eligibility altogether. — [Seller Education Hub 12122](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/12122)

### Inferences
- Fish sauce, oyster sauce and soy sauce in glass bottles are liquids and breakable: not banned by name, but they sit against the "fragile not recommended" guidance and per-country screening. Dried spices could be caught by "plants and related products" depending on how Shopee classifies them; the page does not define the term.
- Dry, sachet-packed seasonings (curry paste sachets, seasoning powders) are the likeliest to pass both the list and the packing guidance. This is a reading of the list, not a confirmed listing.

### Gaps
- No Shopee page was found that says whether liquid condiments are accepted or refused by the SIP logistics leg, or what the weight/size limits are in numbers.
- Destination-country import rules for food sold through Shopee Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines were not researched here.
- I did not verify on the foreign sites whether Thai sauces from Thai SIP shops are actually listed.

## 6. Alternatives when Shopee cannot deliver to the viewer's country

### Takeaway
I could confirm very little from the alternatives' own pages. Lazada Thailand's official affiliate page states up to 12% commission and a 30-day last-click cookie but says nothing about buyers or delivery outside Thailand; Amazon's Associates policies set no residency rule and suggest "#Ad"/"#Sponsored"-style marking. No Thai-origin exporter with both worldwide shipping and an affiliate programme was verified.

### Cited Findings
- [P] Lazada Thailand affiliate page: commission "up to 12%" on orders placed through the affiliate's advertising; cookies kept up to 30 days, last click; commission applies to orders that come from the affiliate's media and are delivered to the customer. The page does not state who may join, payment terms, or anything about buyer location or international shipping. — [Lazada TH affiliate page](https://pages.lazada.co.th/wow/i/th/corp/affiliate-howitworks?hybrid=1)
- [P] Amazon Associates Program Policies: no explicit residency requirement; participants must comply with applicable disclosure rules; the Influencer policy suggests marking posts with "#Ad" or "#Sponsored" where there is a material connection; the Influencer Program is country-specific and needs registration for the applicable Amazon site. — [Amazon Associates policies](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies)
- [S] FlexOffers lists Lazada (TH) at 4% of sales with a 30-day cookie, which conflicts with the "up to 12%" on Lazada's own page (possibly a different tier or outdated). — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/lazada-th-affiliate-program)
- [S] Involve Asia says its Lazada Thailand offer is open to people outside Thailand provided their content reaches a Thai audience. — [Involve Asia](https://involve.asia/blog/lazada-thailand-affiliate-program/)
- [U] Search results indicate Weee! ## 8. How to present the link honestly to an international audience

### Takeaway
The verified facts support a plain statement that the Shopee link is for viewers in Thailand, that the creator earns a small commission, and that viewers elsewhere should buy locally. The platform-side minimum that was verified is TikTok's disclosure setting and YouTube's tolerance of affiliate links with a spam caveat.

### Cited Findings
- [P] The Thai app is usable only within the country of activation and foreign-number sign-up is limited to 10 Southeast Asian countries. — [help article 80892](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/80892); [help article 176286](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/176286)
- [P] Commission is 2.00% direct / 0.60% indirect for Food & Beverages, 7-day last click. — [affiliate article 123898](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/123898); [affiliate article 164346](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/164346)
- [P] TikTok requires the content disclosure setting for third-party promotion in exchange for payment or other incentive. — [TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088170904085)
- [P] YouTube: affiliate content is permitted; creators are responsible for legal disclosure requirements (FTC, ASA cited). — [YouTube Help 9054257](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9054257?hl=en); [YouTube Help 154235](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en)
- [P] Amazon's influencer policy suggests "#Ad" or "#Sponsored" marking where there is a material connection (relevant if an Amazon link is used for non-Thai viewers). — [Amazon Associates policies](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies)
- [P] Shopee's prohibited content includes content unrelated to the product the affiliate link advertises. — [Affiliate T&C, article 77257](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77257)

### Inferences
- Suggested wording consistent with the verified facts (draft, the operator's call): "Shopee link below is for viewers in Thailand only; Shopee Thailand does not deliver abroad. It's an affiliate link, so I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Outside Thailand, look for these brands at your local Asian grocer."
- Do not say or imply "ships worldwide", and do not present the Thai link as usable from Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines; those viewers need their own site's listing.
- Label the link as affiliate in the caption/description on every platform and switch on TikTok's disclosure setting; name the product the link goes to so the content matches the link.
- Keep the linked product identical to the one shown on camera, since commission and Shopee's anti-spam rule both hinge on that match.

### Gaps
- Thai, US (FTC), UK (ASA/CMA) and EU legal disclosure requirements were not read from the regulators' own pages in this pass; only the platforms' pointers to them were seen.
- Whether Shopee Thailand prescribes specific disclosure wording or hashtags for affiliates was not found.
