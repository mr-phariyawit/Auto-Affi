# MathMango (@mathmango1) format study — batch 1 (8 videos)

Captured 2026-11-13, logged out, in the built-in browser. Method: in-page contact sheets (seek the `<video>`, draw frames to a canvas, screenshot), every 0.25 s for 0–2 s, then ~1 s to the end, plus extra frames at transitions and magnified crops for small text.

**Limits of this capture (read first)**
- **No audio.** I cannot hear the clips. Nothing below describes sound. The word-by-word highlighted caption at the bottom is *inferred* to track a voice-over; that is not verified.
- Frames were sampled, not watched continuously. Timings are accurate to about ±0.25 s in the hook and ±0.5 s afterwards. Motion/easing descriptions are inferred from differences between sampled stills.
- Screenshots are ~200 px wide per frame on the sheets; tiny labels were read from magnified crops only where noted. Hex colours are not measured, hues are described by eye.
- Stats are the values embedded in the page at load time (playCount / diggCount / commentCount / shareCount / collectCount). Post dates are from the page's `createTime` (UTC).
- All 8 videos were captured start to end. See "Capture gaps" at the bottom for the few sub-items not read.
