## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. Four elements present: rice, holy-basil pork stir-fry, fried egg, small bowl of prik nam pla.
2. The green leaves read as holy basil leaves, not another leafy vegetable.
3. No long beans, onion or baby corn anywhere on the plate.
4. Fried egg has a crisp browned ruffled edge and a soft (not hard-set) yolk.
5. No spoon, fork, chopsticks, hands or text in the image.
6. Mince looks coarse and fatty-glossy, not fine and dry.
