## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. Two subjects clearly separate with empty paper between them, not touching.
2. Left is a whole egg in shell; right is a single fried egg.
3. The fried egg's edge is visibly ruffled, blistered and browned/crisp — not a soft pale diner-style edge.
4. The yolk reads as soft/runny and domed, not flat and hard-set.
5. No plate pattern, no cutlery, no oil puddle drawn as a separate shape.
6. No text, numbers or labels anywhere in the image.
