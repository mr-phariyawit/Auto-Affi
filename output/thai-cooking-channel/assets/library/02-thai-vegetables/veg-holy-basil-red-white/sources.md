## Deliberately left out (unsourced)

- Fixed leaf length:width ratio, square stem cross-section, calyx colour and shape — no readable source for *O. tenuiflorum* specifically; kept generic.
- "กะเพราป่า" (wild holy basil) — no botanical source; not drawn.
- The pairing white=white flowers / red=pink flowers is an illustrator choice for clarity; the source only states "pink or white". OWNER MUST CONFIRM from a real specimen, or switch both sprigs to the same flower colour.

## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. Leaves come off the stem in opposite pairs, not alternate/staggered.
2. Leaf margins show sparse, widely spaced shallow teeth (not smooth, not finely saw-toothed).
3. Stem and leaves are visibly hairy on both sprigs.
4. Left sprig is pale green throughout; right sprig shows a purple-red flush on stem and veins.
5. The two sprigs are clearly separate with empty paper between them — not merged into one plant, not touching.
6. No text, arrows or labels anywhere in the image.
