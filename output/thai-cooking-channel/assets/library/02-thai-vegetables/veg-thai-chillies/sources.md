## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. Chillies read as small, slim, bird's-eye type — not large spur chillies or bell peppers.
2. Both green (unripe) and red (ripe) fruits are present and roughly equal in number.
3. At least one fruit is split to show the inner seeds and membrane.
4. Fruits are separated with cream paper between them, none touching, countable.
5. Surfaces are matte with no glossy highlight.
6. No text, numbers or labels anywhere in the image.
