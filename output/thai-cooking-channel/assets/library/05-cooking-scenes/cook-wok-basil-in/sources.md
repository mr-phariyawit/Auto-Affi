## Deliberately left out (unsourced / by design)

- Long beans, onion, baby corn — disputed additions, deliberately absent.
- Drawn flames / fire shapes — avoided per brief; only a thin steam wisp.
- Exact chilli cultivar — generic (see veg-thai-chillies).
- Fried egg — belongs to the finished plate (A8), not this in-pan beat.

## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. View is top-down or 45°, the wok filling most of the frame.
2. The basil leaves on top read as fresh, flat and bright green — not wilted, blackened or stirred in.
3. The leaves are recognisably holy basil (elliptical, toothed), not another leaf.
4. The meat looks coarse and browned with pounded chilli/garlic, lightly sauced (not swimming in gravy).
5. No long beans, onion or baby corn; no fried egg; no drawn flames; steam minimal.
6. No hands, no text, no numbers or labels anywhere in the image.
