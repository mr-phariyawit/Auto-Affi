## Deliberately left out (unsourced)

- Added pepper in the mortar — some cooks add "a little pepper" (Krit), but the manifest scopes this card to chilli + garlic, so pepper is not drawn.
- Exact chilli cultivar shape — kept generic (see veg-thai-chillies).
- A finely smooth paste — deliberately avoided; the sourced instruction is a coarse pound.
- Any flame, burner or steam — this is a prep scene, not a hot pan.

## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. A single stone mortar and pestle, centred, on empty paper.
2. The contents look coarsely broken and chunky — not a smooth, uniform paste.
3. Both red and green chilli pieces and pale garlic are visible, with some loose seeds.
4. The pestle is present (leaning on or in the mortar).
5. Minimal/no steam; no drawn flames.
6. No hands, no text, no numbers or labels anywhere in the image.
