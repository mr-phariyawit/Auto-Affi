## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. One wok, centred, seen from directly above, filling most of the frame.
2. The interior is completely empty, clean and dry — no food, oil pool or liquid.
3. The pan reads as dark, matte, seasoned carbon steel (not shiny stainless, not non-stick).
4. Any burner beneath is faint and secondary; no flames drawn.
5. Composition is calm and uncluttered, suitable to layer other elements on top.
6. No text, numbers or labels anywhere in the image.
