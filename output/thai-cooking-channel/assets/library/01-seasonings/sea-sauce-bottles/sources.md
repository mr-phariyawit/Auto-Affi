# Sources — sea-sauce-bottles (draft ref A5)

Four unbranded bottles for the seasonings pillar and the end-card buy list. Brand names are added later in HyperFrames.

## Visual detail -> source

| Drawn detail | Source / tag |
|---|---|
| Four distinct bottle shapes, blank labels | Project brief: generic shapes, not any brand; labels blank so Thai brand text is added in HyperFrames |

## Deliberately left out (unsourced)

- Any brand mark, logo, °N figure, or label text — explicitly blank; distinction is by liquid colour only.
- Exact bottle proportions / cap colours of real brands — shapes are generic by design, "not any brand".
- Protein / nitrogen grade wording — a label matter, not drawn.

## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. Four bottles, four clearly different shapes, standing separated with empty paper between them.
2. Every label panel is blank; no letters, numbers or logos anywhere.
3. Liquid colours are distinct and correct: clear amber / thick dark-brown / thin reddish-brown / near-black.
4. Oyster sauce and dark soy read as thick/opaque; fish sauce and light soy read as thin/translucent.
5. Caps are plain; no brand-coloured or branded closures.
6. Equal scale — no bottle oversized relative to the others.
