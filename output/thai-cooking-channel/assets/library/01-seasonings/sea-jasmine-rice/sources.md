## Deliberately left out (unsourced / not visual)

- Straw-yellow paddy husk — this card shows milled white rice, not unmilled paddy, so the hull colour is not drawn.
- 92% purity rule, KDML 105 / RD15 variety names, GI provinces — label/legal facts, not visual; handled in HyperFrames text with their own checks.
- 2-acetyl-1-pyrroline / aroma compound — not sourced in the spec lines read and not visual.

## Acceptance criteria (check by eye)

1. Two portions clearly separate with empty paper between them, not touching.
2. Raw grains are long, slim and translucent — not short, round or opaque.
3. Cooked rice reads as separate fluffy grains, not a sticky clump or a smooth paste.
4. The raw and cooked rice plainly look like the same long-grain variety.
5. No bowl text, pattern or branding; no cutlery.
6. No text, numbers or labels anywhere in the image.
