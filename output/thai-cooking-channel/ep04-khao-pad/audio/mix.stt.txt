Khao Pad, Thai fried rice served at nearly every street stall. Cook Jasmine rice drier, one cup rice, one cup water. Fry garlic on medium heat until the smallest bits turn golden. Turn the heat up, eggs go in, only half-set. Season with Thai soy sauce, fish sauce, and white pepper. A little sugar balances the salt, it should not taste sweet.
