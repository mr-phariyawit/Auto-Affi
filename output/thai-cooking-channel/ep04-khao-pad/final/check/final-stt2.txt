Khao Pad, Thai fried rice, served at nearly every street stall. Cook jasmine rice drier: 1 cup rice, 1 cup water. Fry garlic on medium heat until the smallest bits turn golden. Turn the heat up, eggs go in, only half set. Season with Thai soy sauce, fish sauce, and white pepper. A little sugar balances the salt. It should not taste sweet. Crab is arguably Thailand's favorite. No crab, use shrimp, pork, or chicken. Always serve lime, add cucumber, and prik nam pla in Thailand. Affiliate links are in the caption.
