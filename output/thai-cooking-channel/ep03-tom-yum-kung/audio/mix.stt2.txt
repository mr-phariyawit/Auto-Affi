Tom Yum Goong, listed as UNESCO heritage in 2024. It began in riverside communities of Thailand's central plains. Three herbs: lemongrass, galangal, makrut lime leaves. Never ginger. Smash the lemongrass. Twist and tear the lime leaves. Keep the prawn heads. Their orange fat adds richness. Add straw mushrooms. Never boil the prawns until tough. Fish sauce seasons it. Lime goes in last, off the heat. Abroad, frozen lemongrass and galangal work. In bio, affiliate links are in the caption.
