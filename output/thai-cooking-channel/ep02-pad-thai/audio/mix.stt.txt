Pad Thai: sour, sweet, salty equal, never mainly sweet. In 1942, Thailand's leader urged Thais to eat noodles. Soak rice noodles in room temperature water for an hour. Sour from tamarind, buy Thai not Black Indian concentrate. Sweet from palm sugar, salty from fish sauce. Pick preserved radish labeled sweet, or it's too salty. Toss until the noodles drink up all the sauce. In Thailand, affiliate links in the caption. Elsewhere, try your Asian grocer.
