## Concepts

### A. Americana street — golden hour (safest, most "artist press photo")
Full body, guitar slung on back by strap, walking past a brick wall, late-afternoon warm side light, shallow DOF. Shows every wardrobe item + boots. Mood: easy, confident.

### B. Studio portrait — dark grey seamless, one key light
3/4 body, seated on a wooden stool, guitar resting upright on thigh, hand on fretboard, looking at camera. Single large softbox camera-left, soft rim. Mood: album-cover serious. Best for showing face likeness.

### C. Backstage / green room
Sitting on a worn leather couch, jacket open, guitar across lap, tuning. Practical tungsten lamp + window daylight mix, documentary feel, slight film grain. Mood: candid, between-sets.

### D. Busker at a BTS/bridge underpass (Bangkok)
Standing with the open guitar case at his feet, strumming, people blurred in background, mixed sodium + daylight. Shows boots and jeans well. Mood: local, relatable, good for สายตำ-style TikTok cover.

### E. Rooftop at blue hour, city bokeh
Leaning on a railing, guitar held by the neck at his side, city lights behind (Bangkok skyline bokeh), cool ambient + warm practical light on face. Mood: cinematic, moody.

### F. Live stage — small club, spotlight + haze
Mid-song, eyes closed, singing into a vintage mic, guitar strap on, top spotlight + stage haze, dark audience silhouettes. Mood: performer energy. Riskier for likeness (eyes closed, angle) — keep for round 2.

### G. Close-up editorial, high-key light grey (matches the JIAB signature-style lighting)
Chest-up, guitar headstock entering frame from below, hands on strings, looking slightly off-camera. Light-grey seamless, big soft key, one catchlight. Mood: clean, commercial, usable as profile/avatar.

### H. Record-store / vinyl wall
Standing among vinyl bins holding the guitar by the body, warm fluorescent + window light, retro colour palette. Mood: music-nerd charm.
