## 2. Functional Requirements (Phase 1)

### FR-01 Product Scout (Shopee)
Scout shall search Shopee TH and return scored `ProductCandidate`s (price band, commission %, rating, sales velocity, novelty vs Wiki). `SPEC.md` §3.1.
**Acceptance**: given a keyword, returns ≥1 candidate with score + reasoning + affiliate-eligible item id.

### FR-02 Strategist → CampaignBrief
Strategist shall produce a `CampaignBrief {product_id, persona, angle, hook, cta, hypothesis, expected_ctr}`, querying Wiki anti-patterns first. `SPEC.md` §3.3.
**Acceptance**: brief validates against schema; cites ≥1 Wiki anti-pattern check.

### FR-03 Writer → Storyboard (HSO×VCS)
Writer shall produce a `Storyboard` (scene-by-scene, Thai-native, ≤60s) meeting the HSO×VCS rubric: hook ≤1.0s, shots 3–5s, captions 100% of dialogue, affiliate-conversion framing (PAS/BAB/UGC). `SPEC.md` §3.4, §6.2, §19.1–19.2.
**Acceptance**: storyboard validates against schema; passes rubric lint (hook timing, shot length, caption coverage).

### FR-04 Producer/Editor → Master video
Producer shall render each scene via the **locked stack** (Higgsfield Seedance 2.0 / veo3_1_lite; Gemini stills; edge-tts Thai VO; HyperFrames captions) and compose a 1080×1920 30fps ≤60s master. **No visible Thai-speaking mouth** — VO over B-roll. `SPEC.md` §3.5/§3.5.1, §19.3.
**Acceptance**: master is 9:16, ≤60s; every dialogue scene is VO-over-B-roll; cost ≤ ADR-004 caps.

### FR-05 Compliance gate (Supervisor) — BLOCKING
Before any publish the system shall verify (`SUPER_SPEC.md`, `SPEC.md` §10):
- Disclosure `#โฆษณา`/`#affiliate` present in caption
- Cleanroom audio: final = exactly 1 audio + 1 video stream; source visual = 0 audio streams
- Speed-guard: Thai VO 1.0–1.15x (warn >1.08x, reject >1.15x)
- Caption/VO sync against approved voice-segment report
- No medical/financial/"guaranteed" claims (claim auditor)

**Acceptance**: a deliberately non-compliant render is hard-blocked with a named reason.

### FR-06 Human approval gates — BLOCKING (ADR-007/008)
No paid provider call before a 3×3 storyboard/contact sheet is shown and approval recorded in `pre_generation_user_review.json`. No public post without human approval. `SUPER_SPEC.md` gates 1 & 10.
**Acceptance**: pipeline halts at storyboard gate and at publish gate awaiting recorded human approval.

### FR-07 Publisher (IG Reel)
Publisher shall post the master to IG Reels with caption/hashtags + affiliate shortlink + subId, and persist a `PublishRecord`. `SPEC.md` §3.6.
**Acceptance**: a real IG post id is recorded (requires G2). Until then: dry-run produces the publish packet.

### FR-08 Analytics + Wiki loop
Analytics shall poll metrics (1h/6h/24h/7d) into a registry; Feedback Curator shall write a `WikiEntry` after outcome labeling. `SPEC.md` §3.7–3.8, §5.
**Acceptance**: one full loop yields ≥1 metrics row and ≥1 Wiki entry.

### FR-09 Run registry + attribution
Every run shall be recorded in the local CSV/JSONL registry with subId attribution (5 levels). `SUPER_SPEC.md` §3, `data/run_registry.csv`.
**Acceptance**: run id, product, artifacts, and subIds are queryable post-run.

## 4. Constraints / Assumptions
- C1: Higgsfield has **no Thai lip-sync** — load-bearing; drives the VO-over-B-roll rule. §19.3
- C2: AI consistency drifts past ~6s/clip and ~8 clips/persona (soul-id cap). §19.1/§19.3
- C3: Live outcome is **gated on G1–G4** (human). Engineering ≠ done. §17.3/§20
