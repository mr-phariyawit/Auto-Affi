## Operating notes

Master identity set: approved logo assets/variants, palette, product silhouette/SKU, material language, tone, and source status. Derivative matrix: deliverable | audience/use | format | master assets | variation | acceptance.

Differentiate conceptual brand/product invention from selling an existing SKU. If inventing a brand, label designs as proposals. If representing an existing product, preserve confirmed geometry and exact label/artwork; do not borrow details from generated variations.

Try-on: inspect fit depiction, grip, anatomy, orientation and stable product. Unboxing: inspect box-open state, contents, size and hands. Website packshot: prioritise readable product. Social/lifestyle: prioritise context while keeping master identity. Motion/stress effects: a dramatic depiction does not substantiate performance.

Deliver the requested variants, master/derivative relationships, and checks. Apply Auto-Affi entry rules when this becomes new paid product production, rather than treating a general brand guide as a model override.
