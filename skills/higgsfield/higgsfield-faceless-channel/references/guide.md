## Operating notes

Topic dossier: audience question, dated research, claim/source ledger, thesis, hook promise. Script: chapter purpose, claim/evidence, example, takeaway, bridge. Scene plan: narration range, visual meaning, reference, reconstruction status, acceptance.

Original insight and coherent narration matter more than imitating a successful channel's identity. Claims about RPM, algorithm preference, monetization and 3-day views are course context. Use current official platform policy when eligibility affects a release.

Full output inspection includes listening, semantic scene-to-script match, repetition, exact labels, and export. Translation is a new timing job with reviewed terminology and pronunciation. Cloning a voice is separate from writing a script.

Packaging variants: one readable focal point, promise/content alignment, phone-size inspection. Shorts: one resolved point, complete context, deliberate crop/subtitles. Schedule iteration around observed retention, not forced daily volume. Do not upload or subscribe automation just because the plan mentions a content calendar.
