## Operating notes

Working artifacts can be one compact operating packet: evidence-based niche hypothesis; offer/deliverables/cadence/revisions; labeled portfolio; intake facts and approval ownership; sample-to-QC-to-delivery states; optional outreach drafts; unit economics.

Intake backward design catches missing contact, service area, offer conditions, price validity, deadline, logo and approver. Keep provisional facts separate from approved wording. When missing facts affect the final output, continue drafting independent sections while keeping dependent claims unresolved.

Outreach: one observed company-specific gap, one relevant proof artifact, one low-friction reply ask. Do not invent lost revenue or pretend the company approved facts from its site. Sending is a separate user-authorized step.

Qualified launch metrics need denominators before outreach. Views and replied interest differ from paid cash and renewal. Subscription value comes from clear output cadence and feedback; economics includes human labor and failed generations. Website implementation, if requested, requires functioning forms and playback, not just screenshots.
