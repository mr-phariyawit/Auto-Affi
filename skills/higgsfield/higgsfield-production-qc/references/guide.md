## Operating notes

Begin with an evidence inventory. Give each reviewed check PASS, ISSUE, NA (irrelevant with reason), or NOT_INSPECTABLE (evidence missing). A face too small to inspect belongs in NOT_INSPECTABLE. A positive field in JSON is declared evidence until actually reviewed.

Issue format: artifact/version + timecode/range; visible/heard observation; approved expectation; severity/blocker; diagnosis hypothesis; one targeted fix; retest. Suggested 0–3 scale is a synthesis rubric, not a vendor benchmark.

Blockers: wrong SKU, unsupported claim, incorrect label/mechanism/CTA, or unplayable output. Other categories: story/payoff, identity, physical contact, geography/state, performance, camera/edit, audio/text, brand and crop/delivery. A high average cannot cancel a blocker.

Keeper format: take ID, in/out, purpose, join constraints, usable audio and caveats. Diagnose repeated defects before reroll. Reuse material only where it doesn't hide a false demonstration or break state.

Cost review: total spend including failed takes divided by approved deliverables; review/integration time separately; currencies never mixed. Delivery check opens the actual exported file. Structural packet checks cannot fill missing visual or audio evidence.
