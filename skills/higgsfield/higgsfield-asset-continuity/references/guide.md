## Operating notes

Use a dependency list before reference creation. Recurring identities and mechanical/brand details deserve locks; one-off background extras may not need full sheets. Select the views actually used. Location reverse views and before/after state sheets are often more valuable than extra beauty variations.

First output a compact register: ID | role | source | version | observed facts | uncertainty | used shots. Then state transitions: beat | before | change | after | receiving shot. Keep real product evidence separate from synthesized design views.

For candidate selection, compare the same action with a fixed prompt while changing person/location only when the experiment needs attribution. Inspect actual motion before asserting a candidate moves well. Specific single-face sheets are a course technique, not a requirement for every possible tool.

Reusable templates are in the director's assets directory (`asset-register.csv`, shotlist and take/QC templates are bundled). If only a sheet is requested, deliver the sheet plus its role/uncertainties; do not force a whole campaign.
